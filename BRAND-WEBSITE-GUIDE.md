# PHARMAVEER Website Brand Guide

## Core positioning
PHARMAVEER PTE. LTD. is a Singapore-based healthcare product development and commercialization company. It develops its own healthcare products and selectively partners with reputable manufacturers and product owners for sourcing, co-development, market entry, distribution and long-term commercialization.

## Brand idea
**Developing Healthcare. Building Trust.**

The website should make PHARMAVEER feel like a focused healthcare company building a long-term portfolio, not a generic trader, dropshipper or catalogue wholesaler.

## Primary audiences
1. Pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, medical-device and healthcare manufacturers.
2. Product owners and innovators seeking Singapore / Southeast Asia partnerships.
3. Clinics, healthcare professionals and institutional buyers.
4. Strategic and commercial partners.

## Brand character
- Clinical, credible and precise
- Modern and ambitious
- Selective rather than mass-market
- Partnership-led
- Premium without appearing luxurious or cosmetic
- Healthcare-aware without making unsupported medical claims

## Visual direction
- Deep navy: authority and healthcare credibility
- Teal: health, progress and clarity
- Restrained warm-gold accent: premium product-development signal
- White / ice backgrounds: clinical cleanliness
- Generous spacing and strong typography
- Avoid stock-photo-heavy pharmaceutical clichés
- Prefer actual product renders, manufacturing imagery and authentic partnership/product-development visuals when available

## Messaging hierarchy
1. We develop healthcare products.
2. We selectively source and evaluate external products.
3. We work with qualified manufacturing partners.
4. We help move suitable products toward commercialization.
5. We value quality, documentation, healthcare relevance and long-term partnerships.

## Public claim guardrails
Until formal processes and evidence are documented, avoid absolute claims such as:
- doctor approved
- doctor certified
- clinically proven
- best / safest / highest quality
- HSA approved unless the exact product and status support it

Preferred language:
- medically informed
- healthcare-professional input where appropriate
- quality-driven
- evidence-informed
- reviewed for healthcare relevance
- subject to applicable regulatory requirements

## Product architecture
Future product pages should use a consistent structure:
- Product name and category
- One-line positioning
- Product visual
- Intended product concept / key features
- Formulation or specifications
- Quality / manufacturing information that can be substantiated
- Intended audience
- Market / regulatory status disclaimer
- Business enquiry CTA

Do not publish a product as commercially available until its status is confirmed.

## Manufacturer partnership page
Future expansion should explain:
- What PHARMAVEER is looking for
- Product categories of interest
- OEM / ODM / private label / licensing / distribution models
- Documentation requested
- Target market discussion
- Partnership enquiry route

## Contact strategy
Use the business email as the primary public contact until a dedicated corporate telephone / WhatsApp number is available. Do not publish a personal number as the main PHARMAVEER contact.
